Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Dawley Asphalt & Paving ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website dawleyasphalt.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it by submitting a consultation request through our contact form. The information you provide — such as your name, contact details, and property description — is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects anonymous data such as pages visited, time spent on the site, general geographic location, and device type. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

reCAPTCHA

Our contact form is protected by Google reCAPTCHA v3, which helps prevent automated spam submissions. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information and sends it to Google for analysis. Your use of reCAPTCHA is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies placed by Google Analytics and Google reCAPTCHA to collect anonymous usage data and prevent spam as described above. We do not use cookies for advertising, tracking across other websites, or any other purpose. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses Firebase (Google) for hosting and form submission handling. When you submit a contact form, your information is transmitted securely through Firebase Cloud Functions and delivered to our team by email. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third parties.

Data Retention

Consultation inquiries are retained only as long as necessary to respond to and fulfill your request. We do not maintain marketing lists or store customer data beyond the scope of your project.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
