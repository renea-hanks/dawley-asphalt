Dawley Asphalt & Paving provides commercial parking lot paving and residential driveway paving in Colorado Springs, CO and surrounding areas. Licensed and insured with over 50 years of experience.

Three generations of asphalt paving service in Colorado Springs has taught us one thing above all else: the ground does not forgive shortcuts. Neither do we. Every project we take on becomes part of a permanent record — one that will be driven on, loaded, frozen, thawed, and judged for decades. We intend to pass that judgment.

I

The Grade

Proper drainage is engineered before a single ton of material is laid. Water is asphalt's primary adversary. We eliminate the path it would take to destroy your investment.

II

The Mix

Every mix is specified for its load, climate, and intended life. Colorado's freeze-thaw cycles demand precision. We do not use a general-purpose answer where an engineered one is required.

III

The Record

Our portfolio is not a collection of photographs. It is a ledger of decisions made correctly under real conditions. Every project we have built still stands as evidence of that standard.
