Commercial Paving Services

Parking Lot Paving

New commercial parking lot installation engineered for load, drainage, and ADA compliance from the ground up. Specification is set before material is ordered. The subbase is prepared before asphalt is laid. There are no shortcuts in the sequence.

Parking Lot Repaving

When a commercial surface has reached the end of its service life, repaving — full removal and replacement — is the correct solution. We assess whether the existing subbase can be retained or must be rebuilt before any proposal is made. Repaving over a failed base produces a failed surface.

Asphalt Resurfacing

Where the existing base remains structurally sound, asphalt resurfacing — an overlay of new hot mix over the prepared existing surface — extends pavement life at a fraction of full replacement cost. The existing surface must be assessed and properly prepared for an overlay to perform as intended.

Asphalt Repair

Potholes, alligator cracking, and isolated subbase failures require targeted removal and replacement — not surface patching that will fail again within a season. We identify the cause before we address the symptom. A patch applied to an active subbase failure is a temporary measure, not a repair.

Commercial Sealcoating

Commercial sealcoating extends pavement life by slowing UV oxidation and reducing water infiltration. Applied on the correct maintenance interval — and to a properly prepared surface — it is one of the highest-return investments in pavement preservation available to a property owner.

Parking Lot Maintenance

A commercial pavement asset managed proactively — annual inspection, timely crack repair, scheduled sealcoating — outperforms one that is ignored until failure by a significant margin in both lifespan and total cost of ownership. We provide assessments and maintenance scheduling for ongoing commercial accounts.
